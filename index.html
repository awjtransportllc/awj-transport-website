About Us

A trusted logistics partner based in Ras Al Khor.

A W J TRANSPORT LLC is a transportation, logistics, and customs clearing company providing complete logistics solutions across the UAE.

We specialize in sea freight container transportation, import and export customs clearance, documentation, port handling, cargo delivery, and business registration support. Our experienced team manages the complete logistics process — from company and account registration for logistics and customs purposes, to customs documentation, port clearance, container transportation, and final delivery. We assist businesses with the procedures and documentation required to establish and operate their logistics and customs activities smoothly and efficiently.
